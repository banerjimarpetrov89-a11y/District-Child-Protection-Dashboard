Dumka District · eCourts Case-Status Records, 2023–2025

District Child Protection Dashboard

Consolidated review of 161 unique cases across 16 police stations, drawn from the Dumka District Court's public eCourts case-status portal — prepared for the Deputy Commissioner and Superintendent of Police, Dumka. Companion to the Version 1 analytical report and Power BI export workbook.

00 / OVERVIEW

District Snapshot

All figures below are computed on the 161 verified unique cases (326 clean raw rows, zero true duplicates) extracted from the Dumka District Court's eCourts case-status search (case types under the POCSO Act and related child-protection statutes, 2023–2025). Registration numbers reset per station and are not a district-wide key — uniqueness was confirmed via Filing No. + Reg. No. + Police Station. As court filings, these are cases that reached the judicial system and may undercount incidents still at FIR/investigation stage.

04 / TREND

Year-wise (YoY) Trend

2022 holds a single legacy entry and is excluded from the trend line. The registered-case count falls ~32% from 2023 to 2025 — read alongside the sharper Kidnapping/Missing drop (29→22→4), this should be verified against FIR and DCPU Missing-Children registers before being read as a genuine decline rather than a 2025 reporting-completeness gap.
